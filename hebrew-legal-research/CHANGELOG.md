# Changelog

All notable changes to this skill are documented here.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added

- references/court-procedure-map.md: which court hears what, the appeal map (as of right or by leave), what binds as precedent, filing and cost pointers, and a worked route for construction-defect disputes (developer vs. hired contractor).
- Sale (Apartments) Law 1973 (inspection and warranty periods, one-year notice, repair opportunity, the 06.04.2011 split) and the Contractor Contract Law 1974.
- Limitation Law Amendment 8 (Swords of Iron suspension) and the settled-land rule (Land Law s.159(b)).
- Local venue (Civil Procedure Regulations reg. 7(a)), the Administrative Court, condominium disputes before the Superintendent of Land Registration, Protection of Wages Law, the pension extension order, Bank Mizrahi (judicial review of statutes), Class Actions Law, unreported-ruling citation form.
- Fourteen glossary terms in the lookup script (now 53); the script now finds terms typed in Hebrew.

### Changed

- Judicial reform rewritten as a dated snapshot (as of 2026-11-03) with check URLs: the elections set for 2026-10-27 and the 14-day convening rule, the reported Mahash interim order, the AG-split and legal-advisers bills under the continuity rule, and the reported Civil Service Commissioner ruling.
- Bar AI ethics: the updated opinion את/60/24 of 2026-09-18.
- Amendment 13: enforcement examples are now cited only from the Privacy Protection Authority's own notices; the NIS 75,000 example, a pre-amendment fine, was removed.
- Contracts Law Amendment 3: two versions of s.25(a) by contract date, "business contract", the unrepresented-party rule.

### Fixed

- Companies Law part map, Contracts Law ss.17-20, Hours of Work (45 hours by statute, 42 by extension order), Severance Pay s.12, Basic Law override and limitation clauses, easement term, docket format (number/year), the HCJ 5100/94 respondent, and the Bar Association Law section that reserves the profession's acts.
