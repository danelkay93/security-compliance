# Domain checklist: Israeli online-store compliance (B2C)

Last reviewed 2026-11-03. Section numbers refer to the Consumer Protection Law (CPL) unless stated.

## Must cover (core)

1. **Who is protected**: B2C only ("צרכן", "עוסק", CPL s.1). Websites AND social-commerce or WhatsApp sales are distance sales (s.14ג(ו)). Covered in the Scope paragraph.
2. **Pre-contract disclosure** (s.14ג(א)), including the address abroad. Step 3.
3. **Written disclosure document** by the time of supply, in Hebrew or the marketing language (s.14ג(ב)). Step 3.
4. **Cancellation windows** for goods, continuing and one-off services (s.14ג(ג)); the extended window only where a conversation took place (s.14ג1). Step 1.
5. **Exceptions**: only the five in s.14ג(ד); never the in-store list of the Cancellation Regulations 2010, reg. 6. Step 1.
6. **Refund mechanics and the fee cap**, with shipping inside the cap, the no-fee cases, and deterioration as damages only (s.14ה). Step 1.
7. **Void clauses**: store credit only, sale items final, tags or packaging conditions, waivers (s.14ה, s.14ז, s.36). Step 1.
8. **Cancellation channels** and the homepage link (s.14ט); termination of continuing transactions (s.13ד(ג)). Step 1.
9. **Unfair standard terms** in the ToS: forum, arbitration, unilateral changes (Standard Contracts Law 1982, ss.3-4). Step 3.
10. **Price display**: total price in shekels, unavoidable fees inside it, exempt-dealer VAT wording (s.17א, s.17ד). Step 2.
11. **Special sales** and the earlier-price period (s.15; 1983 Regulations, reg. 6). Step 2.
12. **Unit price** next to the price online (Unit Price Regulations 2008). Step 2.
13. **No card minimum** (s.12א, Amendment 72). Step 2.
14. **Accessibility**: IS 5568 (WCAG 2.0) Level AA, current exemptions, coordinator, statement, notice-and-cure (Service Accessibility Regulations 2013, regs 35-35ו, 91). Step 4.
15. **Marketing consent** including the existing-customer exception and opt-out (Communications Law s.30א). Step 6.
16. **Enforcement exposure** for refunds (s.31א exemplary damages), accessibility and spam. Steps 1, 4, 6.

## Should cover (advanced)

1. **Gift vouchers**: minimum validity and expiry marking (s.14ח). Step 3.
2. **Credit notes** where no refund is due (s.14ז). Step 1.
3. **Misleading advertising**: fake reviews, undisclosed paid posts, false scarcity timers, reference prices (CPL ss.2, 7). Deferred to the next cycle.
4. **Warranty certificates** for listed products (Warranty and After-Sale Service Regulations 2006) and non-conformity remedies (Sale Law 1968). Deferred.
5. **Hebrew marking of goods** (CPL s.17 and the marking order). Deferred.
6. **Price errors** and conflicting displayed prices. Deferred.
7. **Installment and credit sales** disclosure (1983 Regulations). Deferred.
8. **Call recording** for phone or outbound sales (Amendment 74, s.16ד, not yet in force). Deferred until in force.
9. **Tourism sold at a distance** and performed abroad (s.14ג2). Deferred.
10. **Administrative fines and class actions** (CPL s.22 onward; Class Actions Law 2006). Deferred.
11. **Privacy pointers**: collection notice, Data Security Regulations 2017, transfers abroad. Routed to israeli-privacy-shield.
12. **PCI DSS v4.0.1** as a contractual acquirer obligation. Step 2.
13. **Accessibility extras**: the registered-users exemption (reg. 35ו(ה)) and video captioning (reg. 35ד). Deferred.
14. **Fixed-term and auto-renewing deals** (CPL s.13א) and price-chapter exemptions (s.17ז(א)). Deferred.
15. **Marketing after a subscription ends** (Communications Law s.30א(ד)(2)). Step 6. A prudent "פרסומת" label on SMS too: deferred (case-law trend, not statute).
16. **Physical-shop return-policy notice** (CPL s.4ג), mentioned in Step 1 as a pointer; the in-store regime itself is out of scope.

## Out of scope (explicit)

- **Product legality** (cosmetics notification, mandatory standards, toys, electrical goods, product liability): separate regulatory regimes; the skill flags them and routes to the regulator or counsel. Reviewed 2026-11-03: users do ask, but an accurate answer needs per-category regulatory research this skill cannot carry.
- **Food, supplements, alcohol**: use israeli-food-business-compliance.
- **Deep privacy program** (privacy officer, database duties, security procedures): use israeli-privacy-shield.
- **Tax registration, VAT returns, bookkeeping**: accounting domain.
- **Medicines, medical devices, tobacco, weapons, age-restricted goods**: special statutes; flag and refer to counsel.
- **B2B wholesale stores**: the CPL does not apply.
- **Foreign law** (EU, US) for stores selling abroad: different regime.
- **Live litigation or regulator proceedings**: needs a licensed advocate.
